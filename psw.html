How to Choose the Right Solar Company in Peshawar

  • Size the battery to your feeder, not a template. Outage hours vary hugely across Peshawar's feeders. Demand a design based on your actual load-shedding pattern and the loads you need carried through it — this decision drives both cost and satisfaction.
  • Verify equipment provenance. Ask for panel serial numbers, the flash-test report, and proof of manufacturer warranty registration. Karkhano-sourced B-grade stock sold as new is Peshawar’s most common solar scam.
  • Confirm PESCO net metering handling. PESCO is the slowest major DISCO — 8 to 14 weeks on a clean file. Confirm the company files end-to-end in-house and has live applications in the system right now.
  • Ask about hail. Spring hailstorms hit the Peshawar valley regularly. Confirm the panel's IEC 61215 impact certification and that the mounting angle and warranty terms account for hail.
  • Insist on monitoring. A hybrid system has more failure modes than on-grid — battery degradation, charge-controller faults, transfer-switch issues. Real-time monitoring with alerts is the difference between a fix and a dead system discovered during an outage.
  • Check references in your area. Hayatabad, DHA, University Town, and Warsak Road have different roof stock and feeder behaviour. Ask for references in your specific area and call them about year-two service response.
