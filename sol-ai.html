How Sol AI Works

Sol AI connects directly to your inverter and pulls data refreshed every ~10 minutes — generation output, consumption patterns, grid import and export, and system health metrics. It cross-references this data with local weather conditions to identify underperformance that is not explained by cloud cover or seasonal variation.

For residential systems, Sol AI tracks your self-consumption ratio — the percentage of solar energy you use directly versus what you export to the grid. Under Pakistan's new net billing rules, maximizing self-consumption is the key to maximizing savings. Sol AI shows you exactly where those opportunities are.

For commercial installations, Sol AI provides multi-site dashboards, string-level performance analysis, and automated daily reports delivered via email and WhatsApp. Your O&M team gets actionable alerts, not raw data.

Sol AI is bundled with every new Solar Citizen installation for an initial period — after that, monitoring continues as a paid subscription. Standalone monitoring is also available for systems we did not install.
