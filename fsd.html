How to Choose the Right Solar Company in Faisalabad

  • Match the installer to the project class. Mill-scale industrial, SME loom-unit, and residential rooftop are three different disciplines. Ask for references in your specific class — a great house installer is not the right choice for a sawtooth shed roof.
  • Confirm FESCO net metering handling. FESCO is efficient by DISCO standards, but commercial applications above 25kW involve load studies. Confirm the company files end-to-end in-house and has done your application class before.
  • Demand winter-honest yield estimates. Smog season cuts central Punjab generation from November to January. If the proposal only shows an annual average, ask for the monthly curve — an installer who won’t show it is hiding it.
  • Get a soiling and cleaning plan. Industrial particulates mean Faisalabad panels need cleaning every 4–6 weeks. Ask how the mounting design allows access and whether a cleaning contract is offered.
  • Compare the bill of materials, not the bottom line. Faisalabad’s negotiating culture rewards the cheapest quote — and the cheapest quote is where B-grade panels and undersized cabling hide. Read the BOM like you’d read a cloth contract.
  • Insist on monitoring. Soiling and smog losses are gradual. Without real-time string-level data you won’t see the slide until the FESCO bill arrives. Ask which platform is included and whether it alerts on faults.
