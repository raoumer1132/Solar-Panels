HOW WE WORK

Six Stages From Your Last Bill To Your 25-Year Warranty

Each stage has a hard deliverable and a typical timeline. No vague promises — specific paperwork, specific numbers, specific dates.

1
DAYS 1–2

Bill assessment & load profile

We read your last 6–12 months of K-Electric / LESCO / IESCO / FESCO / MEPCO / PESCO / HESCO / QESCO bills, build a kWh load profile, and size the system to your actual consumption ±5%. We tell you the right size, not the biggest one.

Deliverable: written sizing report
2
DAYS 2–4

Expert Survey & roof assessment

Our engineering team measures usable roof area, checks structural mounting feasibility, inspects the electrical panel and runs a shadow analysis. For complex roofs and industrial sheds we deploy drone imagery for a precise shadow map — so if your roof can't take the system you want, you find out before any down-payment changes hands.

Deliverable: roof + shadow + panel report
3
DAYS 4–7

Engineering design

String layout, inverter sizing, cable cross-section, breaker sizing, surge-protection, earthing scheme and net-metering paperwork — issued as a stamped engineering drawing your bank or building society recognises. Our designs are accepted by DHA Karachi, Bahria Town, Lahore Cantonment and Islamabad CDA at no extra cost.

Deliverable: stamped engineering drawings
4
DAYS 7–10

Proposal, pricing & financing

A single PKR-explicit quote with every line item priced in Pakistani Rupees — no surprise add-ons at commissioning. Need financing? We coordinate the bank application with Meezan, Bank Alfalah, Faysal, JS or HBL at current KIBOR-linked rates, and flag any concessional programme you qualify for.

Deliverable: PKR-explicit quote + bank coordination
5
DAYS 10–18 / 30–60 COMMERCIAL

Installation & net-metering filing

Tier-1 panels mounted, inverter wired, DC + AC sides commissioned and performance-tested under load. We then file the bidirectional-meter paperwork with your DISCO — at no extra cost, regardless of utility — and send a written status update every two weeks until approval lands (typically 4–8 weeks).

Deliverable: commissioned system + filed net-metering app
6
DAY 1 — YEAR 25

SOL AI activation & Sol Care

Every system goes live on SOL AI from commissioning day: WhatsApp fault alerts, monthly performance summaries and tariff-aware export optimisation. From year two, Sol Care handles annual electrical inspections and panel cleaning, and we pass through the Tier-1 manufacturer's 25–30 year performance warranty.

Deliverable: lifelong monitoring + maintenance
